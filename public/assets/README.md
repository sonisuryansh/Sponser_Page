## 2. Still-Needed Real Production Media Checklist

To transition from the initial presentation to full school launch collateral, the following high-resolution real photos and short clips should be captured on-site:

1. **School STEM Lab Photography**:
   - Wide-angle landscape photo of the active school lab room with students at tables (daylight or bright interior lighting).
   - High-angle top-down photo showing the safety-marked indoor drone flight hoop circuit.
   - Clean photo of the kit storage cabinet showing labeled shared kit compartments.

2. **Student Project Work per Grade (High-Res Photos or 10-15s Silent MP4 Loops)**:
   - **Grade 4**: Students interacting with visual block-coding and simple physical design builds.
   - **Grades 5-6**: Breadboard circuits, motor drivers, and basic sensor connections.
   - **Grade 7**: Microcontroller screen showing Python console output and live sensor data.
   - **Grade 8**: Autonomous wheeled rover navigating an obstacle course on the lab floor.
   - **Grade 9**: Student capstone team presenting their 3-axis arm or IoT device to a teacher.
   - **Grade 10**: Interactive data dashboard demonstrating analysis of classroom environmental logs.

3. **Software & Learning Platform**:
   - Full-fidelity screenshot or recording of the MedhaArc Labs student dashboard, showing student progress across modules, books, and quizzes.

---

## 3. Production Specs
- **Images**: WebP format, dimensions around 1920×1080 (hero/full) or 1200×800 (cards), compressed under 200 KB.
- **Videos**: MP4 (H.264), 10–15 seconds, silent/muted, looped, under 2 MB, accompanied by a matching `.webp` poster frame.
